# Desktop Pet

It's a replica of my dog that can live on your desktop

## What She Does

- She wanders around your window
- She stops at random and idles, sits, naps, rolls over, and barks
- She barks when you drag her
- She stays on the top window so you can always see her

## How To Run

Download the .exe file from https://hihih1hi.itch.io/my-desktop-pet and once downloaded double click the file and when you want to get rid of her you click on her and press Alt+F4
